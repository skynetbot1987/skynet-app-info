Skynet

A personal video-production and analytics tool.

What this app is

Skynet is a private, single-user tool that the operator runs on their own computer. It helps them plan, render and review their own short-form videos, and — with the operator's explicit permission — read back a summary of the performance of videos published on their own TikTok account.

It is not a public service, it has no other users, and it does not act on anyone else's account. The TikTok integration is used only by the account owner, on their own account, for their own reporting.

What it accesses, and why

  • user.info.basic — the operator's own display name, avatar and follower count, shown on their private dashboard.
  • video.list — the operator's own published videos and their public metrics (views, likes, comments, shares), shown on the same dashboard.

No data from any other TikTok account is requested, collected or processed.

Privacy Policy

  1. What is collected. Only the data described above, read from your own TikTok account after you grant access: your profile basics and your own videos' public metrics.
  2. Where it is stored. On the operator's own computer, in local files. There is no Skynet server and no Skynet database.
  3. Who it is shared with. Nobody. The data is not sold, rented, published, or transmitted to any third party.
  4. Credentials. TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the operator's machine and are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's own API.
  5. Retention and deletion. Data is kept only while the operator uses the tool. Deleting the local files removes it permanently, and access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account's Manage app permissions settings.
  6. Children. This tool is not directed at, and is not used by, anyone under 18.
